# Minimalism: `/design style minimalism`

I remove everything the task does not need. Space, one type family and one accent carry the interface.

---

## Required companion reference

I follow the [style workflow](style.md) for report context, scope, implementation and verification. The command loads it alongside this guide.

---

## The design pass

- I use a neutral canvas, one text color and one accent reserved for actions and the current state.
- I use one type family with two or three sizes per screen. Hierarchy comes from size and space. Clean product sites I checked each use a single sans, and some add a mono face for labels.
- I pick one corner radius for the whole product. The sites I checked range from 6px for tools to 12px for cards to full pills for friendly brands. I do not mix them.
- I separate sections with 1px rules at very low contrast, or with space alone. I avoid cards and shadows that add nothing.
- For buttons, a 2px bottom edge with no blur gives a tactile press without decoration.
- I keep icons at one size and stroke weight. I remove any element that does not help the task.
- I test long text, 320px width and 200% zoom. Empty space must not hide content or push controls off screen.
