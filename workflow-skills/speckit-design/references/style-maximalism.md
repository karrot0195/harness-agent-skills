# Maximalism: `/design style maximalism`

I fill the interface with color, pattern, type and imagery. A clear hierarchy keeps the density readable and makes it look intentional.

---

## Required companion reference

I follow the [style workflow](style.md) for report context, scope, implementation and verification. The command loads it alongside this guide.

---

## The design pass

- I choose a bold palette of four to six colors and a strong pattern or texture. Each color has one job: surface, accent or text.
- I mix two or three type styles with clear size steps. The headline wins the eye before anything else.
- I group related content into blocks with thick borders or color fields so the density has structure.
- I keep the primary action and the main content area calm, with fewer competing patterns.
- I check that every piece of text sits on a surface with enough contrast, including patterned backgrounds.
- I test 320px width and 200% zoom. Dense blocks must reflow into one column without overlap or horizontal scrolling.
